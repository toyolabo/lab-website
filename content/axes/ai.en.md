We work broadly across modern AI modeling — large language models, generative AI, and foundation models. Rather than simply using existing models, we design from both sides of capability and evaluation: how to build human preferences and behavior into models, how agents should act autonomously, and how to verify their reliability.

The AI pillar merges with the graph pillar into graph foundation models, and with the HPC pillar into large-scale training infrastructure. It is also the core of applied topics such as recommender systems and neuroscience.
