Most phenomena in the world can be written as connections between points: atomic bonds in molecules, correlations between brain sensors, relationships between people and products — all of these are graphs. Centered on graph neural networks (GNNs), we study the theory and technology for extracting knowledge from this structure.

We work at scales from millions to billions of edges. Our strength shows precisely in the challenges invisible on small graphs — training efficiency, generalization, and dynamic change. Combined with the AI and HPC pillars, graphs extend into applied research in materials informatics, neuroscience, robotics, and more.
