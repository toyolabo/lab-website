At Suzumura Lab, we refine high-performance computing techniques on supercomputers and mdx (a platform for building data-empowered society), developing the techniques and software that enable billion-edge graph processing and distributed GNN training.

Refining large-scale computing techniques through our research, while also helping operate the infrastructure and services that support research, underpins the speed and scale of our applied research. From speeding up graph processing and distributed GNN training to operating platforms and services such as mdx, this pillar supports AI and graph research from the systems side.
