Suzumura Lab actively pursues joint research with companies and research institutions, built on our three core technologies: graphs, AI, and HPC. Techniques for decoding the structure of large-scale data apply across finance, manufacturing, materials, healthcare, and many other industries.

Our laboratory has carried out joint research with a wide range of companies. Going forward, we will continue to actively pursue joint research, both on putting our knowledge and research results into practice in society and on fundamental research in related fields. If you are interested, please feel free to contact us by email (suzumura [at] acm [dot] org).

## Case: Traffic forecasting with spatio-temporal meta-graph learning
article: graph-traffic
image: images/collab-traffic-forecasting.png

In joint research with Toyota Motor Corporation, we developed the Meta-Graph Convolutional Recurrent Network (MegaCRN), which learns road-network traffic flow as a spatio-temporal graph to forecast traffic with high accuracy. We also built a new large-scale traffic-speed dataset covering 1,843 road links of the Tokyo Metropolitan Expressway, and showed that the model adapts to abnormal situations such as accidents (AAAI 2023).

Related link: [Publications of our research partner (Toyota) ↗](https://www.toyota-tokyo.tech/publications/)

## Case: News recommendation that accounts for news avoidance
article: rec-awrs
image: images/collab-rec-awrs.png

In joint research with Nikkei Inc., we developed AWRS (Avoidance-Aware Recommender System), which treats readers' deliberate choice not to read news (news avoidance) as a core element of recommendation. It consistently outperformed existing methods on news datasets in three languages, including Japanese (SIAM SDM 2025).

## Case: Job recommendation in healthcare and nursing care with reinforcement learning

In joint research with SMS Co., Ltd., we are bringing reinforcement learning into a job recommendation system that connects care providers with job seekers holding various qualifications and skills, to make matching more efficient (37th Annual Conference of JSAI, 2023).
