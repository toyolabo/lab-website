The options for joining our laboratory as a student are as follows.

## 1. Master's program

Our laboratory belongs to the Department of Information and Communication Engineering, Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, The University of Tokyo. If you wish to enter the master's program, please apply and take the entrance examination according to the department's admission guidelines.

## 2. Doctoral program

Admission to the doctoral program also follows the department's admission guidelines, as with the master's program. However, if you wish to apply for the doctoral program, please be sure to contact Prof. Suzumura (suzumura AT acm.org) in advance with your research achievements and research plan. In addition to regular doctoral students, we also accept working professionals pursuing a doctorate. Scholarships such as Spring GX are available.

[Spring GX](https://www.cis-trans.jp/spring_gx/index-e.html)

## 3. Research student

A research student is an enrollment status for studying and conducting research on a specific topic under a faculty member's supervision, without directly aiming for a degree. Enrollment is normally for one year, during which you may also prepare for the master's or doctoral entrance examinations.

[Research students at The University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/prospective-students/research_students.html)

## 4. Joint research

If you are a student belonging to another laboratory at The University of Tokyo or another university and wish to conduct joint research with us, you must first obtain approval from your supervisor. We will then consider accepting the collaboration only when an agreement on joint research with Suzumura Lab has been reached.

If anything is unclear, please contact Prof. Suzumura.

## Contact

For inquiries about joining the lab, please email the lab office (replace AT with @).
