Professor at the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology and the Information Technology Center, The University of Tokyo. Previously at IBM Research Tokyo, IBM Research Dublin, IBM T.J. Watson Research Center, and the MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab; professor at UTokyo since 2021. Ten-time world #1 on the Graph500 supercomputing ranking; ACM RecSys'23 Best Paper Runner-Up Award; board member of JSAI. Leading research from HPC through graph deep learning to graph foundation models.
