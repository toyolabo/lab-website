summary: Two papers were accepted at IEEE EMBC 2025: GEFM, a graph-enhanced EEG foundation model, and a domain adaptation model that bridges differences between EEG datasets from different sites.
---

Two papers from our lab have been accepted at IEEE EMBC 2025, the 47th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (Copenhagen).

“GEFM: Graph-Enhanced EEG Foundation Model”
Limin Wang, Toyotaro Suzumura, Hiroki Kanezashi

“Domain Adaptation Model for EEG Analysis: Mitigating Spatial and Spectral Variability in Heterogeneous Datasets”
Hisashi Ikari, Toyotaro Suzumura, Shotaro Akahori

[IEEE EMBC 2025](https://embc.embs.org/2025/about/)
