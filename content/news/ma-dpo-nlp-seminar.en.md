This is Yu Tomita, a second-year Master's student at Suzumura Lab.

At the 268th IPSJ SIG on Natural Language Processing, I presented "Extending Direct Preference Optimization to Recommendation Based on Multi-Faceted Understanding of User Preferences with Large Language Models."

![](images/hero-3.jpeg)

This work proposes Multi-Aspect DPO (MA-DPO), which builds the multi-faceted preferences written in user reviews — genre, location, price range, and so on — directly into an LLM to achieve more accurate recommendations. Experiments on real-world Yelp and Amazon Video Games data confirmed accuracy gains over existing methods that do not use review information.

The talk prompted many questions on the method's details, experimental setup, and future directions, leading to lively discussion. Next steps include addressing the cold-start problem and validating on larger models and datasets.
