We recently held a reunion for the alumni of Suzumura Lab!

![](images/gallery-suzulab-2026-10-06.jpg)

Suzumura Lab was also active at Tokyo Institute of Technology (now Institute of Science Tokyo) from 2009 to 2013, and on this occasion, alumni from that period got together with the current members of the lab at the University of Tokyo.

Hearing from our seniors who are active in a wide range of fields was very inspiring, and it was a valuable opportunity to build new connections across generations.

Suzumura Lab is looking for students to do research with us. If you are interested in joining the lab, please see the "[Join Us](#/join)" page.
