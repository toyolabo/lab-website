From September 1 to 4, 2026, the 44th Annual Conference of the Robotics Society of Japan (RSJ2026) was held at the Natural Science and Technology Main Hall, Kakuma Campus, Kanazawa University. From our laboratory, second-year master’s students Shota Onohara and Yankai Wang, as well as research student Minghao Gong, participated in the conference and gave oral presentations.

Shota Onohara presented “Evaluation and Training-Free Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action Models for Deployment with Heterogeneous Grippers,” proposing an adaptation method that mitigates the performance degradation of VLA models caused by changing only the gripper through simple interventions at inference time.

Yankai Wang presented “LabMate: A Safety-Aware Human–Robot Collaboration Benchmark for Laboratory Environments,” proposing an evaluation methodology and execution framework that enable robots to ask humans for clarification when instructions are ambiguous and to refuse or stop execution when a task is unsafe.

Minghao Gong presented "ShadowVLA: Rescuing Frozen VLA Skills under Out-of-Distribution Object Placements by Trajectory Transfer," proposing a method that transfers a frozen VLA's successful trajectories to new object placements, recovering failed behaviors without retraining.
