summary: We held the kick-off meeting of the University of Tokyo team in the Watt-Bit Collaboration demonstration project of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications. We will demonstrate shifting computation across resources such as mdx to maximize power efficiency.
---

Today we held the kickoff meeting for the University of Tokyo team in the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications' FY2026 "Watt-Bit Collaboration Demonstration" program.
As the rapid expansion of AI drives up data center demand and power consumption, "Watt-Bit collaboration," which optimizes power and communication infrastructure in an integrated way, is becoming increasingly important.

![](images/IMG_6327.jpeg)

In this project, the UTokyo team will demonstrate "advanced workload shifting": moving computation itself both spatially (choosing which computing resource to use) and temporally (choosing when to run) across geographically distributed resources such as the academic cloud platform "mdx," supercomputers across Japan, and data centers, in order to maximize power efficiency.

As a concrete use case, with an eye toward contributing to "AI for Science," we will build on our AI inference service "mdx-MaaS" and demonstrate a range of workloads from simple AI inference to autonomous task execution by multi-agent systems.

Those who lived through the grid computing era may feel a sense of déjà vu, but this time is different: there is strong momentum and collaboration from the power (watt) side, together with AI as a powerful application and the large market it brings. With social implementation in mind, we are determined to make this project a success!
